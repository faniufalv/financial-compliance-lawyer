## Q1: [Foreign Investment] What legal considerations should overseas Chinese have when buying property in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese purchasing property in China should note: 1) Confirm purchase eligibility, some cities have restrictions; 2) Overseas income certificates need notarization and authentication; 3) Fund remittance requires foreign exchange registration; 4) Property registration should use Chinese name plus passport number.

**法律依据**: 《不动产登记暂行条例》

**领域**: 房产纠纷 | **语言**: English
