## Q1: [Criminal Defense] What legal risks should overseas Chinese be aware of when investing in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese investing in China should be aware of: 1) Foreign Investment Negative List restrictions; 2) Foreign exchange controls requiring compliance approval for profit repatriation; 3) Tax compliance considering China tax law and bilateral tax treaties. We recommend engaging professional lawyers for comprehensive legal due diligence before investment.

**法律依据**: 《外汇管理条例》

**领域**: 涉外投资 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Cross-border E-commerce] What is insider trading crime and how to defend against it?

**A**: Insider trading crime refers to insiders or those illegally obtaining inside information trading securities before information is public. Defense points: 1) Whether information qualifies as statutory inside information; 2) Whether defendant is statutory insider; 3) Causal relationship between trading and inside information.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国刑法》第180条

**领域**: 证券犯罪 | **语言**: English
